About

Mark
Majcherczyk-Olczak

Mark Majcherczyk-Olczak is a London-based filmmaker, visual effects artist, and Founder & Creative Director of Majcherczyk Digital, an independent production company developing original film, television, and digital content.

The Work

Working across development, production, and post-production, Mark combines storytelling with visual effects and creative technology to bring original projects from the page to the screen.

His interests span a variety of genres and forms, with a focus on creating distinctive stories and visual worlds. While visual effects remain an important part of his creative skill set, filmmaking and storytelling sit at the centre of his work.

Original Work

Mark is best known for Project Nova (2025), an original animated feature project, and #NotHer (2027), an upcoming live-action feature film.

Through his work, he continues to develop original films and television projects while building the creative relationships and production foundations needed to bring them to life.

Before Majcherczyk Digital

Mark's creative journey began in 2025 with a focus on building and selling websites, developing an early foundation in digital creation and entrepreneurship.

During the same period, he returned to Blender for the first time since around the age of twelve. Revisiting 3D and digital filmmaking reignited his interest in visual effects, animation, and creating cinematic worlds.

After producing several short films, he began developing Project Nova, an ambitious original animated feature project. The experience became an important step toward his wider filmmaking ambitions and eventually led toward the development of Majcherczyk Digital.

Majcherczyk Digital

Mark founded Majcherczyk Digital to create a place where filmmakers, artists, and other creatives can come together to build something great.

As Founder & Creative Director, he works across the company's creative direction and original projects, bringing people and ideas together across development, production, and post-production.

The long-term vision is to build Majcherczyk Digital into an independent production company capable of developing original work across film, television, and digital media.
